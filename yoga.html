Practice notes

Yoga beyond the pose

Traditional yoga includes physical practice, breath work, attention and disciplined observation. The goal of a session does not always need to be intensity.

Start with consistency

A shorter practice performed regularly can be more useful than an occasional session that leaves you exhausted.

Use the breath as feedback

When breathing becomes strained or attention becomes scattered, reduce intensity, slow down or rest. The breath can help you notice when you have pushed beyond the quality of the practice.

Respect progressions

Advanced positions and breath retention practices deserve progressive learning and, where appropriate, guidance from an experienced teacher.

Red Legendary principle: Yoga is not only about how the body looks. It is also about how clearly you can observe and control your practice.
