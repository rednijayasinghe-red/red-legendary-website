Timing

Speed is not only how fast you move; timing is choosing when to move and when not to.

JKD & martial arts principles

Train the movement before chasing the speed

Many martial arts skills become easier when the body can maintain balance while changing direction, range and rhythm.

Footwork basics

  • Keep a stable base and avoid unnecessary crossing of the feet.
  • Practice forward, backward and lateral movement with control.
  • Use small steps when precision matters; larger steps only when the situation allows.

Distance awareness

Build drills around entering, touching the edge of range, exiting and resetting. This gives technique a context instead of turning training into isolated movements.

Speed with control

Increase speed only when your position, balance and mechanics remain reliable. Fast mistakes are still mistakes.

Red Legendary principle: Skill should make movement simpler, not more complicated.
