Training framework

A simple way to build a stronger program

Start with a small number of movements you can perform consistently. Track the work, then improve it gradually.

1. Choose your goal

Muscle growth, strength, conditioning, body composition and performance can overlap, but your primary goal should guide your exercise and volume choices.

2. Make technique repeatable

Use a range of motion and execution you can reproduce from session to session. Quality reps are easier to progress than chaotic reps.

3. Track the work

Record sets, reps, load and effort. A training log turns “I think I’m progressing” into evidence you can review.

Red Legendary principle: More exercises do not automatically mean more progress. Better structure usually beats random volume.
